# Retail Sales & Product Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive 3-page **Power BI** dashboard analyzing retail sales, gross profit, profit margins, returns, store tiers, regional distribution, and product brand performance.

## 📌 Dashboard Structure & Key Features

### 1. Cover Page (`cover`)
- Clean landing page with quick navigation buttons to explore the report sections.

### 2. Sales Overview (`Sales`)
- **KPI Cards with Trendlines & MoM Tracking:**
  - **Total Sales:** `1.76M`
  - **Gross Profit:** `1.05M`
  - **Profit Margin:** `60%`
  - **Total Returns:** `8K`
- **Sales & Gross Profit by Store Type:** Compares performance across `Supermarket`, `Deluxe Supermarket`, `Gourmet Supermarket`, `Mid-Size Grocery`, and `Small Grocery`.
- **Monthly Trend Analysis:** Tracks monthly fluctuations in Total Sales and Gross Profit from January to December.
- **Customer Performance Table:** Displays `Sum of Quantity`, `Total Gross Profit`, `Total Returns`, and `Total Sales` per customer.

### 3. Product & Regional Performance (`Product`)
- **Total Quantity & YoY Progress:** Tracks `833K` units sold and YoY sales progress.
- **Regional Sales Breakdown:** Highlights top regions (`North West`, `Mexico Central`, `South West`, `Canada`, etc.).
- **Top Stores by Quantity:** Ranks top-performing branches (`Store 13`, `Store 17`, `Store 15`, etc.).
- **Product Brand Performance:** Compares Gross Profit and Total Sales across brands (`Best Choice`, `High Top`, `Hermanos`, `National`, etc.).
- **Monthly Profit Margin Trend:** Tracks profit margin stability across the year (`59.5%` – `59.9%`).
